🌀 TRICK ROOM

A big empty room and a box of tricks. Build something that fools people — then walk into it and get fooled by your own room.

👁 Building

You float about and see the truth. Fakes are outlined, portals are plain frames, and the magic spots are marked on the floor.

🚶 Walking

You're down on the floor at eye height, and everything looks real. This is the only place the tricks actually work.

An illusion only ever works from one pair of eyes, at one height, in one spot. That's why there are two ways of looking, and one button between them.

Whatever you build is still here next time — the room is written down when you change it, and comes back when you do.
