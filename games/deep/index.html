🏝 DEEP BLUE

You wake up on a little island in the middle of the sea. There are palm trees to chop for wood, and a sunken ship down there with metal you can prise off it.

Build a workbench, then make all four bits of gear — flippers, a diving lamp, an air tank and a copper helmet — and the whole deep opens up to you.

Watch your air. Pop up to the top for a big breath, or find the bubbles hidden down in the deep. Run out and you just float up — nothing else happens.

W A S D to move · Space to jump, and to kick up in the water · Shift down · E to chop, build and make · drag the mouse (or the arrow keys) to look · B for the Sea Book

Left stick to move · ⬆ ⬇ to go up and down · the button above the air bar does whatever you're standing next to · swipe anywhere to look round
