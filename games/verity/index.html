VERITY

Verity is a little ball of light with a smile, and she is very good at helping.

You're lost in six dark rooms. Verity floats along beside you and lights the way, and shouts if you're about to tread on something spiky.

Press ASK VERITY and type her a question — anything you like — and she'll answer you out loud. Ask where to go and she'll work out the way and show you. She never runs out and she never minds being asked.
