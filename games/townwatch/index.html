🏘 TOWN WATCH

Five people live in this town. When it gets dark, things come up the four roads looking for them, and you are the only one awake.

You can't be everywhere at once — so you build. Walls to slow them down, turrets to see them off, and both only where you think they're coming.

Pick what you start with. All three are worth exactly the same — ninety wood's worth — they just spend it differently.
